This article was submitted to Zenn’s “Share Your OpenTelemetry Knowledge” writing contest.

It describes an experiment in which a local LLM running through Ollama was given interfaces that looked identical but used different HTML structures. I then measured the differences in how the LLM interpreted them using OpenTelemetry and Grafana.
KOKOCHI UI, a personal project currently in progress, was named after the Japanese word for “comfortable” because I want to **provide comfortable interfaces that are easy for both people and AI to use**. The article covers my experience building an environment for gathering evidence to support that project’s premise.
