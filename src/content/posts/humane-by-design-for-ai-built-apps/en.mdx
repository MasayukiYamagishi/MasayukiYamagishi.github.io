Humane by Design is a project by Jon Yablonski, who is well known for Laws of UX. Its name uses the word “humane,” meaning compassionate and considerate toward people, and the website offers a way to think about **design intended to respect people**.

Before I wrote this article, I could not find any articles about Humane by Design on Zenn, so I wanted to introduce this excellent project.

## Key takeaways

- As AI lowers the barrier to whether a web application can be built, deciding whether it should be built and how it should behave becomes increasingly important.
- Humane by Design focuses on user well-being and asks whether a design is beneficial to people.
- Its principles often conflict with real-world requirements. Rather than treating them as rules that must always be followed, I view them as a tool that adds useful perspectives when making product decisions.

## Recommended for

- Engineers working on web applications and similar products
- People who want to create products that work well for a diverse range of users
